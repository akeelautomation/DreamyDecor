Create a cozy reading nook with a woven pendant light

A well‑designed living room balances comfort with style, and a focused reading zone can make evenings feel more inviting. By choosing the right lighting and accessories, you can create a functional space without crowding the room.

The key is to place a pendant light where it illuminates your favorite chair while keeping the view of the window clear. Simple measurements and thoughtful placement turn a plain corner into a welcoming retreat.

Quick win
Place a woven pendant light directly above the armchair, measuring the chair width plus 12 inches for the ideal diameter and hanging it 30 inches above the highest point of the chair.

1. Assess the space and define the reading zone

Start by measuring the width of the armchair and adding 12 inches to determine the appropriate pendant diameter. This ensures the light feels proportional without overwhelming the seating area.

Mark a spot on the wall or ceiling directly above the chair where the light will hang, keeping at least 30 inches of clearance from the top of the chair back to the fixture.

2. Choose the right pendant style and finish

A woven pendant adds texture and a natural feel that complements most living room palettes. Opt for a finish that matches existing wood tones or metal accents for cohesion.

Consider the height of the ceiling; a lower ceiling may require a shorter chain, while a taller ceiling allows for a longer drop, creating a more dramatic focal point.

3. Install with a dimmer for versatile ambiance

Wiring the pendant to a dimmer switch lets you adjust brightness for reading, relaxing, or entertaining. This flexibility makes the nook usable at any time of day.

If you’re not comfortable with electrical work, hire a licensed electrician to ensure safe installation and proper code compliance.

4. Complement with layered textiles and accessories

Add a round jute rug that extends 12 inches beyond the front legs of the chair, providing a soft base and defining the zone without blocking traffic flow.

Place a small side table 18 inches from the armrest for a lamp, book, or cup, keeping essentials within easy reach while maintaining visual balance.

5. Avoid common clutter pitfalls

Keep decorative baskets on the floor rather than on the chair or side table to prevent visual clutter near the reading spot.

Use sheer linen curtains instead of heavy drapes to let natural light filter in while preserving privacy, which enhances the brightness of the pendant.

6. Check lighting direction and reflectivity

Position a mirror on the wall opposite the window so it bounces daylight toward the pendant, amplifying the warm glow and making the nook feel larger.

Ensure the mirror’s angle reflects light toward the window rather than away, maximizing the combined effect of natural and artificial illumination.

Checklist

  • Hang pendant 30 inches above the highest point of the chair
  • Install a dimmer switch for adjustable lighting
  • Select a woven pendant that matches existing finishes
  • Place a round jute rug extending 12 inches beyond chair legs
  • Position side table 18 inches from chair arm
  • Keep woven basket on floor to avoid clutter
  • Use sheer linen curtains for light and privacy
  • Set up a mirror to reflect light toward the window
